REFERENCE-MATERIAL COVERAGE CONTRACT

Read this entire file after the permanent package contracts. This specialized
contract controls mapped-reference coverage but never enlarges the locked formal
target or relaxes the whole-unit, ownership, proof, language, or body-only rules.

READ THE ASSIGNED MATERIALS

Read `reference_materials/REFERENCE_INDEX.json` and every listed part completely
before drafting. The Markdown part is a page-ordered navigation and OCR layer, not
authority for mathematical symbols. For a PDF part, use its split PDF pages as the
visual source of truth whenever a formula, heading, diagram, table, subscript,
superscript, quantifier, domain, or proof step is uncertain. Do not infer content
from an omitted source or an OCR placeholder.

When the index names `REFERENCE_OUTLINE_PROMPT.txt`, read that persistent contract
before planning. Its application-approved hierarchy and physical-episode identity
rules control outline interpretation.

For a math lecture, ChatGPT Work also screens the full course book list for the
target topics and reads relevant unmapped passages. Compare the primary exposition
with the reference books and author notes before deciding whether they overlap.
Capture actual page anchors, decisions and the beginner teaching plan in
`SOURCES.json` before drafting. Relevant examples and intermediate calculations
can deepen a topic already in scope.

The recording's corresponding primary-textbook range bounds each episode.
Reference reading must not move a later chapter's or section's proof into this
episode merely to complete prerequisites. State the full result needed, its
hypotheses and verified source, check its application, and identify the
corresponding later chapter or section where the detailed proof is deferred.
Record the named result, destination and reason in SOURCES.json reading.note.
Current-range proofs, local lemmas and intermediate computations remain detailed.

THE MATHEMATICAL-OWNERSHIP INTERSECTION

A listed reference part is eligible evidence, not an instruction to copy the whole
part. The writable reference contribution is the intersection of:

- content present in the listed reference part;
- content belonging to the exact application-selected formal target;
- content supported by the current recording/content contracts or explicitly named
  in `reference_mapping.decisions[].coverage`;
- content not already fully owned by accepted previous notes, except for minimal
  restatement or genuine revisiting.

Every proposed reference item must satisfy all four conditions. A complete source
page is indivisible as a file but divisible by mathematical ownership. Shared or
boundary pages may therefore appear in adjacent packages without making all of
their mathematics writable in both. Read the whole page for context, but include
only the current target's owned contribution.

If the mapped part contains the tail of a previous unit, the body of the current
unit, or the beginning of a later unit, exclude the neighboring material. If an
earlier accepted unit already owns a definition, theorem, proof, construction, or
major example, cite or minimally restate it unless the present mapping explicitly
assigns a genuine strengthening, a revisited proof, or formal development of an
earlier preview.

FIGURES FROM REFERENCES

When available, read the course-wide TEXTBOOK_CATALOG.json through the registered
prepare_online_course_textbook_catalog operation. Its Work-reviewed figure groups
compare all selected primary and supplementary sources by mathematical meaning.
Choose figures that help the current lecture; retain complementary alternatives,
avoid redundant repetitions, and do not insert all candidates automatically.
Unreviewed candidates and extraction failures remain explicit. The catalog is a
navigation and selection aid, never a substitute for reading the assigned section.

MinerU-derived parts may list `figure_context_file`,
`reference_diagram_obligations_file`, and `figure_asset_files`. Read the listed
context and inspect each source crop. A crop or description is evidence, not final
artwork. Apply the same ownership intersection and the permanent drawing contract.
The user's textbook-coverage instruction assigns the corresponding textbook
definitions, results, proofs, examples and original figures even when the lecturer
uses a different order. Cover these items substantively, with detailed derivations,
rather than replacing them with a summary. Keep a page-to-topic mapping so later
textbook content is covered in its appropriate course unit.
Prefer the actual original textbook figure over a newly drawn imitation. Use the
indexed hash protocol for original PDF crops; preserve native vector content and
original raster pixels without resampling or artificial enhancement. Inspect the
crop, explain it in nearby prose, and credit its actual source and printed page.
Do not invent a primary-textbook attribution for a supplementary-book figure.

INTEGRATION AND FINAL COVERAGE PASS

Use the recording Agent's board notes, transcript, outline, and exposition choices
as evidence for order, notation, and presentation. Reconcile eligible reference
content with those sources instead of appending a source-labelled textbook section.
Preserve the application-owned outline and complete selected formal unit. Physical
recording ranges and reference-file boundaries are never output parts.
If the package says that Section is the finest outline level, do not create a
Subsection or any lower formal outline node.
Do not merely summarize eligible reference content or cite the source in place of
writing the target-owned mathematics.

Write the final LaTeX entirely in polished academic English. For Chinese source
prose and captions, translate their mathematical content into English; preserve
mathematical notation. Do not copy Chinese prose or retain Chinese characters in
the final LaTeX. Do not output source IDs, filenames,
mapping reasons, page-coverage labels, or a checklist.

Before returning, privately inspect every listed part and confirm that every item
inside the four-way intersection appears substantively, while every item outside
it is excluded. Never import an unlisted part or use an entire mapped section merely
because the package contains it.
