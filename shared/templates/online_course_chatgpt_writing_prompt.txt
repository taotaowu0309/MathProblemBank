MATH PROBLEM BANK PERMANENT CHATGPT AUTHORING CONTRACT

PRIORITY AND REQUIRED READING

This is the highest-priority package-level authoring contract for every formal
online-course Section or Subsection. Read this entire file and every permanent
companion contract named by `ChatGPT_PROMPT.txt` before planning or drafting.
`ChatGPT_PROMPT.txt` is only the package-specific dispatcher: it identifies the
locked target, available materials, and read order, but it does not duplicate or
replace the permanent rules in this file.

EXACT WRITABLE SCOPE AND MATHEMATICAL OWNERSHIP

Return one complete, integrated LaTeX body for the exact application-selected
formal writing unit. Recording segments, timestamps, evidence blocks, textbook
parts, continuity files, and conversation history are sources, not independent
writing scopes. Content is writable only when it belongs to the locked target
under the current recording/content contracts or an explicit reference mapping.
Continuity alone never makes previously written material writable again.

Before drafting, privately create a CONTINUITY COLLISION LEDGER. For every
substantial definition, theorem, proof, construction, and major example that may
appear, identify its principal ownership location and classify the proposed use as:

- already established;
- minimal restatement needed;
- new strengthening;
- new proof revisited;
- preview becomes formal theory.

Every substantial mathematical object should have one principal ownership
location in the course. If it is already established, cite it or restate only the
minimum needed for the current argument. Reproduce a complete proof or construction
only for a genuine current strengthening, an explicitly revisited proof, or a
preview that the locked target now develops as formal theory. Never repeat a result
merely because the current evidence or mapped source mentions it again.

WHOLE-UNIT EXPOSITION

Obey `WHOLE_SUBSECTION_WRITING_PROMPT.txt`. Integrate all in-scope evidence before
writing and organize the unit by mathematical dependency and teaching logic, not
recording chronology or source order. Preserve every distinct supported
hypothesis, definition, result, proof step, calculation, example, exercise,
warning, and explanation that is owned by the target; remove genuine overlap.
Read `LECTURE_ERRORS_AND_CORRECTIONS.md`, verify every reported diagnosis and
correction, and independently check the complete target for additional mathematical
errors. Never preserve a demonstrably false claim merely because it appears in a
source.

Use the supported semantic environments truthfully. Definitions, results,
standalone examples, exercises, mathematical remarks or warnings, and target-owned
proofs use their matching environments. Motivation, transitions, contextual
claims, and connective reasoning remain ordinary prose. Never turn a contextual
or motivational claim into a boxed theorem merely to make it look formal.

PROOF OWNERSHIP

Apply the five-category decision rule in `UNEXPANDED_PROOFS_PROMPT.txt` to every
nontrivial result before deciding whether to prove it. Complete proofs are required
for current target results and explicit current obligations. Previously established
results are cited or minimally restated, not proved again. External prerequisites
are stated in the exact version needed but are not proved unless current recording
evidence, explicit mapped-reference coverage, or an evidence obligation assigns
that proof to this target. Contextual claims remain prose. The mere use of a
`theorem`, `lemma`, `proposition`, or `corollary` environment does not override
this ownership decision.

Every retained example must still contain the calculation or verification needed
to establish what that example claims. Definitions must state complete hypotheses,
object types, operations, and conditions. Do not manufacture a proof obligation
for a definition, a motivational observation, or an already completed earlier
result.

CORE NARRATIVE AND OPTIONAL SUPPLEMENTS

Mathematical completeness does not require every long technical proof to interrupt
the first-reading path. State the core definition or theorem before a long
target-owned technical supplement whenever mathematically legitimate. Mark the
supplement clearly with a short first-reading remark, place supporting lemmas with
it, and resume the main conceptual thread immediately afterward. Advanced material
is retained when it is owned by the current target, but it must not obscure the
unit's core narrative.

When the unit invokes an external theorem, state the exact version and hypotheses
needed before using it. Do not silently invoke an unspecified theorem, and do not
expand the external theorem into a new chapter-length proof unless its proof is
assigned to the current target.

FORWARD-SCOPE CONTROL

The end of the unit may contain a short transition explaining what the next formal
unit will do. Do not systematically develop the next unit's definitions, theorems,
constructions, examples, or proofs here. A brief preview does not transfer principal
ownership into the current target.

LANGUAGE, OUTPUT, AND IMPORT SAFETY

Write entirely in polished academic English. Translate Chinese speech, OCR,
headings, examples, and reference text while preserving mathematical notation.
Return only compilable body LaTeX. The application owns and inserts the formal
outline. Obey every command, environment, paragraph, proof-marker, diagram, and
image-path rule in `LATEX_IMPORT_CONTRACT.txt` and `LATEX_DRAWING_RULES.txt`.

EXERCISE AND SOLUTION CONTRACT

Every exercise statement owned by the target must have exactly one complete solution
immediately after it. Never write "left to the reader", "the proof is similar",
"obvious", "routine", a hint instead of a solution, an answer-only line, or a
placeholder.

For a standalone exercise use:

\begin{exercise}
<complete exercise statement>
\end{exercise}

\begin{solution}
<complete detailed solution>
\end{solution}

For a numbered exercise set inside a top-level `enumerate`, keep each statement
and solution in the same `\item`; put `\textbf{Solution.}` alone on its own source
line and begin the answer in the following paragraph. A detailed solution must
justify every nontrivial implication and treat all relevant cases and boundary
conditions.

REFERENCE, CONTINUITY, AND DRAWINGS

Read `REFERENCE_AND_CONTINUITY_PROMPT.txt` on every formal writing task. When
reference files are present, obey the specialized coverage contract and its
mathematical-ownership intersection. When continuity files are present, read the
complete prior lecture PDF before drafting, use it to maintain notation and locate
prior ownership, and never copy its wrappers or rewrite earlier content.

Read `LATEX_DRAWING_RULES.txt` completely. Keyframes and screenshots are evidence
only. Include a new TikZ figure only after its mathematical purpose, diagram type,
object types, and relation to the surrounding prose pass that contract's private
gates. When curated diagram materials assign a realization marker, preserve it
immediately after the matching complete drawing; a marker without its drawing is
invalid. Zero figures is a valid result.

PRIVATE FINAL CHECK

Before returning, check the complete draft against all permanent contracts and the
private whole-unit plan and collision ledger. Confirm that every substantial item
has a defensible ownership classification; no earlier proof, construction, or
example has been repeated without a genuine current reason; the core narrative is
visible before optional supplements; external prerequisites are stated at the
right strength; no later unit has been developed early; and all explicit current
proof obligations are resolved. Also confirm that the output is one complete
body-only target unit, every required atom is substantively realized, no forbidden
command, Chinese character, evidence timestamp, or unfinished marker remains, and
every emitted figure is present, explained, and mathematically faithful. Return
only LaTeX; do not print the plan, ledger, or check.
