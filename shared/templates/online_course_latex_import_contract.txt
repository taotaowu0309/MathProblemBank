PERMANENT LATEX IMPORT CONTRACT

Read this entire contract before writing. It mirrors the deterministic structural
checks performed by the Math Problem Bank online-course LaTeX importer. These are
hard requirements, not stylistic suggestions.

1. ORDINARY SELECTED-UNIT MODE IS BODY-ONLY

The normal workflow has already selected and locked the target Chapter, Section,
and Subsection in the application. Return only that target's body.

- Do not output `\setcounter`.
- Do not output `\part`, `\chapter`, `\section`, `\subsection`,
  `\subsubsection`, `\paragraph`, or `\subparagraph`.
- Do not repeat, translate, shorten, or restate the selected unit title.
- Begin directly with mathematical prose or the first legitimate semantic
  environment.

Historical files may contain compatible legacy headings. They are read-only
evidence and are never templates for ordinary import. Only when a prompt explicitly
declares `LEGACY_OUTLINE_IMPORT_MODE` may legacy headings appear. In that exceptional
mode they must be a single contiguous prefix at the absolute beginning, occur at
most once each, follow `chapter` then `section` then `subsection`, and use titles
identical to the application-locked titles. `\setcounter` is not allowed even in
that prefix. No ordinary selected-unit prompt enables legacy mode.

2. SUPPORTED SEMANTIC ENVIRONMENTS

Use these environments whenever the mathematical content has the corresponding
meaning:

- `kind=definition` -> `definition`;
- `kind=theorem` -> `theorem`;
- `kind=lemma` -> `lemma`;
- `kind=proposition` -> `proposition`;
- `kind=corollary` -> `corollary`;
- `kind=example` -> `example`;
- `kind=exercise` -> `exercise`, followed immediately by `solution`;
- a mathematical remark, convention, or warning -> `remark`;
- a supplied proof -> `proof`, immediately after the result it proves.

The importer recognizes `definition`, `theorem`, `lemma`, `proposition`,
`corollary`, `example`, `exercise`, `remark`, `proof`, and `solution`. A generic
`kind=statement` must be classified by its actual mathematical role: use the
appropriate environment if it is a named semantic result, otherwise write it as
ordinary prose. A `kind=proof_step` belongs inside the corresponding proof or
explanation and does not justify inventing a separate result.

Never simulate Definition, Theorem, Lemma, Proposition, Corollary, Example,
Exercise, Remark, or Proof with `\textbf`, `\textit`, `\emph`, `\noindent`,
`\paragraph`, or a plain prose label. Do not create bold or italic topical
headings. Motivation, transitions, background, and connective explanation stay
as ordinary prose paragraphs.

Before returning, inspect every explicit mathematical semantic object and verify
that it is wholly contained in the correct environment. A marker or environment
name without the actual mathematical content does not count.

3. FORBIDDEN DOCUMENT-LEVEL AND ACTIVE COMMANDS

Return body content only. Do not output:

- `\documentclass`, `\begin{document}`, or `\end{document}`;
- `\usepackage`;
- `\input` or `\include`;
- `\write18`, `\openout`, `\read`, or `\immediate`;
- `\newcommand`, `\renewcommand`, or `\def`;
- shell commands, file-writing commands, external URLs, or absolute paths.

Do not wrap the answer in Markdown fences and do not include a preamble, audit
report, checklist, explanation of the response, or summary of your work.

4. DETERMINISTIC CONTENT REJECTIONS

- The body must contain no Chinese character.
- The body must contain no evidence timestamp such as `[00:12:34]` or a Markdown
  timestamp heading.
- No `【待补证明：...】` marker may remain.
- Every required proof obligation must be completed in the mathematics. Proof
  IDs and `% evidence-obligation: ... resolved` comments are optional
  bookkeeping and never establish mathematical completion.
- Every required Mathematical Atom must appear substantively before its matching
  `% MPB-ATOM: <atom-id> realized` comment. Never emit a coverage comment alone.

5. DRAWINGS AND EVIDENCE IMAGES

Never emit `\includegraphics`. Follow `LATEX_DRAWING_RULES.txt`. Every new
non-textbook figure must use its single LaTeX-safe `% MPB-DIAGRAM-SOURCE-BEGIN`
protocol with `backend=tikz` and exactly one complete body-local `tikzpicture`.
Penrose, Quiver/tikz-cd, Asymptote, Graphviz, CeTZ, and raw `tikzpicture` content
outside that block are rejected. The application compiles and inserts the derived
vector PDF during import. Agent-final keyframes, board screenshots, and page
renders remain evidence only. Generated assets are application-owned under
`generated_diagrams/` or `textbook_figures/`; never write or guess a path there yourself.

The sole image exception is an indexed original textbook diagram. Refer to it
only with `% MPB-TEXTBOOK-FIGURE: sha256=... width=...`; never emit its path. The
application accepts only a hash present in the current material package, copies
the original bytes unchanged, and verifies the hash after copying.

6. PARAGRAPH AND EXERCISE STRUCTURE

Separate genuine prose paragraphs with a blank line. Do not imitate paragraph
breaks with `\\`, `\newline`, or repeated `\noindent`. A numbered exercise set
may use one top-level `enumerate`. Apply the exercise/solution rules from
`GLOBAL_CHATGPT_WRITING_RULES.txt` exactly.

7. PAGE-BREAK-SAFE BODY LATEX

The semantic environments are breakable `tcolorbox` environments. Do not emit
`\newpage`, `\clearpage`, `\pagebreak`, `\nopagebreak`, `\vfill`, or manual
vertical skips to control pagination. Begin a long theorem, proposition, example,
or proof with a short prose sentence before the first display, and separate a
sequence of displayed formulas with explanatory prose paragraphs. Do not put a
single giant paragraph containing many consecutive `$$...$$` or `\[...\]` blocks
inside one semantic box. Split such material into logically complete paragraphs
so the box can break at ordinary paragraph boundaries. A proof may follow an
example immediately after `\end{example}`; this is valid, but the example itself
must still contain its stated construction or calculation. The generated PDF must
not contain an interior page whose only text is running header/footer furniture.
